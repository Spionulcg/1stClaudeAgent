## Positioning Tips

- **Lead with shipped titles** — "Senior Game System Designer with 2 shipped mobile titles" in your headline
- **Specialise your pitch** — mention progression systems, economy design, live ops balancing, monetization (these are $$ keywords for mobile studios)
- **Case studies over CVs** — for each title, show: the problem → your system → the result (retention %, D1/D7, monetization lift if available)
- **Target mobile-first studios** — mid-size studios (10–50 people) with live games need contract system designers most; they can't afford full-time specialists
- **Mention EU timezone** — EU clients actively prefer EU-based contractors for overlap
